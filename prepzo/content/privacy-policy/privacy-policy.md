Material we generate from an uploaded file may, however, be reused for another user who uploads an identical file. This applies only to byte-for-byte identical files and only to material generated by Service. We keep that generated material for as long as at least one user still holds the corresponding file; once no user does, it is deleted. See section 8 of our Terms and Conditions.

You can delete Your Uploads at any time from within Service, which also removes the stored file and retires the content generated from it. Deleting your account removes Your Uploads and their derived content.

11. Your Rights Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") applies to our
processing of your personal data. Under it, we are the Data Fiduciary that
determines how your personal data is processed, and you are the Data Principal
to whom that data relates.

  11.1. Basis for processing. We process your personal data on the basis of
  the consent you give when you create an account and use Service, and for the
  legitimate uses permitted by the DPDP Act, such as processing data you
  voluntarily provide for a purpose you have not objected to. We ask for your
  consent through a clear notice and process your data only for the purposes
  described in this Privacy Policy.

  11.2. Right to access information. You may ask us for a summary of the
  personal data we process about you, the processing activities we undertake,
  and the identities of any other Data Fiduciaries and processors with whom we
  have shared it.

  11.3. Right to correction and erasure. You may ask us to correct inaccurate
  or misleading personal data, to complete incomplete data, to update it, and
  to erase personal data that is no longer needed for the purpose it was
  collected for. You can correct most of your details yourself in your account
  settings, and you can delete your account and its associated data from there
  at any time.

  11.4. Right to withdraw consent. You may withdraw your consent at any time,
  as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness
  of processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you withdraw consent for
  processing that is necessary to provide Service, we will stop providing
  Service to you and delete your account data, subject to any retention we are
  required by law to apply.

  11.5. Right of grievance redressal. You may raise a grievance with our
  Grievance Officer, whose details are in section 21. We will respond within
  the timelines set out there. You are required to raise your grievance with us
  before approaching the Data Protection Board of India.

  11.6. Right to nominate. You may nominate another individual to exercise
  your rights under the DPDP Act on your behalf in the event of your death or
  incapacity. Contact us to record a nomination.

  11.7. Your duties. The DPDP Act requires you not to impersonate another
  person when providing personal data, not to suppress material information
  when providing it, and not to register a false or frivolous grievance.

  11.8. Complaints. If you are not satisfied with our response to a grievance,
  you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
